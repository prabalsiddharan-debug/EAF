ABOUT EAF

Entrepreneurs Awareness Forum

EAF was born from the Karnataka State Commercial Tax Officers Association (KSCTOA), with a focus on encouraging entrepreneurs to gain knowledge, understand GST compliance and grow their businesses through structured networking.

01

GST Education

Creating awareness and educating business persons regarding GST norms and changes.

02

Business Promotion

Promoting and facilitating business opportunities through networking and direct referrals.
